# Prior-work check: decorative zoom / crop substitution (2026-10-25)

## Verdict: our agent audit is largely scooped. The adversarial-selection + advocate-defense part is still open.

**Wang et al., "The Illusion of Visual Tool-Use: A Causal Audit of Thinking with Images", arXiv 2608.06270 (Aug 6, 2026)**
- Models: DeepEyes, PixelReasoner, Mini-o3, Qwen3-VL-4B/8B, Thyme. Benchmarks: V*, HR-Bench 4K/8K, VisualProbe, MME-RealWorld-Lite.
- Same intervention as ours: online replacement of returned crops with random crop / noise / blank, plus a step-level counterfactual.
- Same findings: DeepEyes and Thyme ≈ 0 drop; Mini-o3 and Qwen collapse by 50–64 pp. "Calling without looking" (Mode 1) dominates DeepEyes (72.8%); the pre-call think already commits to the answer in ~58% of cases.
- They also have a force-answer experiment: Mini-o3 never commits at the tool-call limit, which matches our 70% abstention.
- Not covered: adversarial or attacker-chosen crops, security of the selector, any defense or fix.

**Liu et al., "On the Faithfulness of Visual Thinking", arXiv 2510.23482 (Oct 2025)**
- DeepEyes and PixelReasoner on V* and HR-Bench: corrupting the visual part of the multimodal CoT changes accuracy by <4%, while corrupting the text changes it by 8–24%.
- Proposes a fix (the SCCM reward).

**Also related:** CodeV (CVPR 2026; faithful tool use via tool-aware PO), DeFacto (2509.20912; counterfactual thinking with images).

**Adversarial selection:**
- No paper found that attacks the crop/zoom selector of a thinking-with-images agent or of a WSI MIL pipeline with selected crops.
- The closest work is multimodal agent prompt-injection (Wu et al. 2406.12814; CrossInject 2504.14348). That work perturbs pixels or injects instructions; it does not choose which real region is shown.
- No WSI "selector hijacking" or debate/advocate MIL paper found either (shallow search).

## Implication for CVPR
- Drop "decorative zoom" as a headline contribution. Cite Wang 2026 and Liu 2025 for the reliance spectrum and use our audit only as a confirming table.
- Our confabulation numbers (75–90% "confirms" on blank crops) are a small addition, not a paper.
- Headline should go back to: **evidence selection is an attack surface** (real, unmodified regions chosen adversarially; no pixel perturbation). The agents that do rely on crops (Mini-o3, base Qwen, WSI MIL) are exactly the ones that can be hijacked. The defense is per-hypothesis advocates or the hybrid presenter: robust in pathology (3 seeds, adaptive attack) and in VLMs (nway .81/.84 V*).
- Missing for that story: an adversarial-crop attack on Mini-o3 itself. It is load-bearing, so does it get hijacked or does it abstain? Plus an advocate wrapper for an agent.
