# CVPR 2027: honest odds (2026-10-25, draft v0 = paper/cvpr/main.pdf)

## Bottom line
- **Accept odds as of today: about 15–25%.** If the must-fix items below are done and the writing is strong, maybe 25–35%.
- The CVPR base rate is about 22–25%, so this paper is around average, not a favourite.
- This is a solid MICCAI paper (pathology half alone) and a plausible CVPR borderline.

## What reviewers will like
- Simple, clean threat (real regions only, no pixels), shown in two very different domains with one mechanism.
- The negative result is the most interesting part: advocates/debate fail exactly at the true advocate. Debate people will care.
- Statistics are careful: 3 seeds × 5 folds, paired CIs, adaptive attacker, every slot attackable.

## What will get it rejected (most likely first)
1. **"Is this a realistic threat?"**
   - Who compromises a crop selector? Reviewers may call it contrived.
   - Need a concrete motivation in the intro: third-party or outsourced triage models, model-supply-chain swaps, a tool API controlled by another party, a buggy selector.
   - This paragraph is the biggest risk.
2. **"The method is trivial."**
   - The hybrid is "advocates + one more crop", and Prop. 1 is a pigeonhole argument.
   - Defense: the paper's value is the finding plus the evaluation, not the architecture. Frame it as an analysis paper with a simple fix.
   - Some CVPR reviewers accept that; some don't.
3. **Unequal budget.**
   - The hybrid shows one more slot; in pathology hybrid-K encodes 2K tiles.
   - Need: single selector at 2K tiles, clean (cheap to run on Narval before Oct 27, or after Nov 3).
4. **Unequal attacker.** The single selector was attacked with the fixed attacker. **Fix running now** (Narval jobs 7299072/85/90).
5. **Natural-image judge is training-free and uses letter scoring.**
   - No generation, no trained VLM judge, and only 191 + 200 questions.
   - A reviewer may say "toy". HR-8K or MME-RealWorld would help; a judge trained against the attacker would help a lot but is too much work for 3 weeks.
6. **Agent section overlaps with Wang et al. 2026.** Kept as context and cited; the Mini-o3 hijack is new but small.
7. **Hybrid still loses .14–.24 under attack.** We say "degrades instead of collapsing". Some reviewers will want more.
